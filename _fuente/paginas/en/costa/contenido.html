Destinations

Where do you want to go?

Three landmark spots on the Chilean coast, just 1.5 hours from Santiago.

Viña del Mar

The Garden City. Beaches, a casino, gardens and seafront restaurants.

Valparaíso

A UNESCO World Heritage site. Painted hills, Victorian funiculars and port-city character.

Isla Negra

Pablo Neruda's house facing the Pacific. A literary landscape and fresh seafood.
