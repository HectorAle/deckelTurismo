How it works

01

Book ahead

Send us your date, the resort, how many of you there are and whether you are bringing your own gear.

02

Confirmation and details

We confirm your driver, vehicle and departure time. We check the snow report and adjust the route if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. There is enough room for skis, snowboards, poles and boots. If your group is carrying a lot of gear, tell us when you book so we assign the right vehicle.
We follow the snow report and the access conditions in real time. If the road to a resort closes, we tell you straight away and reschedule at no cost.
Nevados de Chillán is in the Ñuble Region, roughly 480 km south of Santiago — about six hours down Route 5. It is not a same-day round trip like the resorts above Santiago, so we arrange it as a multi-day trip or a one-way transfer. Send us your dates and we will put the plan together.
In high season (July and August) we recommend booking at least 48 hours ahead, as demand is high. Outside those months, 24 hours is usually enough.
